Caliber vs Fitbod

Fitbod generates each workout for you based on your goals, equipment and recovery. Caliber runs a structured program in six-week blocks, or the spreadsheet program you bring, and adjusts each set to how you are doing. Pick Fitbod for variety without planning; pick Caliber for a structured strength program.

Where Fitbod is the better choice

  • You want fresh, varied workouts without following a fixed program.
  • You train for general fitness more than strength on specific lifts.
  • You are on Android today.

Where Caliber is the better choice

  • You want a structured program with blocks that build on each other.
  • You already have a spreadsheet program you trust.
  • You are training for a meet or another date.
  • You want loads to match the plates and dumbbells you own at home.

Who should pick which

Fitbod is a good fit if you want the app to choose your exercises each day. Caliber is a better fit if you care about steady progress on your main lifts over months, inside a plan you can see.

Frequently asked questions

Is Caliber cheaper than Fitbod?

At current prices, yes: $79.99 a year for Caliber versus $95.99 a year for new Fitbod subscribers.

Does Caliber change exercises every session?

No. Caliber keeps your program’s exercises stable across a block so progress is measurable, and swaps only when your location or equipment requires it.

Does Caliber work for home workouts?

Yes. Save your home equipment, heaviest dumbbells and plates, and Caliber adapts the same program when you train there.
