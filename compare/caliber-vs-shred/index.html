Caliber vs Shred

Shred offers coach-built plans tuned by AI, studio-style classes and social challenges, and adapts to the equipment where you train. Caliber focuses on strength: it runs a periodized program or your own spreadsheet, adjusts every set from RIR or RPE, and keeps one program progressing across gym and home.

Where Shred is the better choice

  • You want studio-style classes alongside strength training.
  • You like friend challenges and leaderboards.
  • You want to start on a free tier.

Where Caliber is the better choice

  • You want a strength program you can see and plan around, not just today’s workout.
  • You already run a spreadsheet program.
  • You want home loads matched to your exact dumbbells and plates.
  • You are training toward a meet or event date.

Who should pick which

Both adapt to where you train. Shred leans toward variety, classes and social motivation. Caliber leans toward a structured strength program that keeps progressing wherever you lift, at a lower annual price.

Frequently asked questions

Do both apps adapt to home equipment?

Yes. Shred adapts workouts to the location you choose. Caliber adapts one program across your saved locations, including your heaviest dumbbells and the plates you own.

Which is cheaper?

At current prices, Caliber’s annual plan is $79.99 and Shred’s is $119.99. Shred also has a limited free tier.

Does Caliber have classes?

No. Caliber is built for strength training, with guided zone 2 and interval cardio sessions.
