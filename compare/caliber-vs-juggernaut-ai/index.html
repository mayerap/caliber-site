Caliber vs Juggernaut AI

Both apps adapt your training to your feedback, from set to set and block to block, and both can plan toward a meet. Juggernaut AI is powerlifting-focused, with a community and coach extras. Caliber costs much less, runs your own spreadsheet programs, and adapts one program across gym and home.

Where Juggernaut AI is the better choice

  • You are a competitive powerlifter who wants a meet day advisor and attempt planning.
  • You value a coaching community and live coach Q&A.
  • You are on Android today.

Where Caliber is the better choice

  • You want adaptive programming at a fraction of the price.
  • You want to run your own spreadsheet program, not only a generated one.
  • You split training between a gym and a home gym.
  • You want guided cardio alongside your lifting.

Who should pick which

If powerlifting meets are the center of your training and the coaching community matters, Juggernaut AI is built around that. If you want adaptive, periodized strength training for less than a quarter of the price, Caliber covers the core.

Frequently asked questions

Is Caliber a Juggernaut AI alternative?

For many lifters, yes: both adapt loads to your feedback and plan blocks toward a date. Juggernaut AI adds powerlifting-specific extras and a coaching community.

How much cheaper is Caliber?

At current prices, Caliber’s annual plan is $79.99 versus $349.99 for Juggernaut AI.

Can Caliber peak me for a powerlifting meet?

Yes. Set the meet date and Caliber plans backward, ending with a peak.
