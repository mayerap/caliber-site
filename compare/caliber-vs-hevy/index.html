Caliber vs Hevy

Hevy is a popular workout logger with a large community and a low-cost Pro plan. Caliber is a strength-training app that runs your program, adjusts loads set by set and builds the next block when the program ends. Choose Hevy to log and share; choose Caliber to be coached through a program.

Where Hevy is the better choice

  • You write your own routines and mainly want a fast, reliable log.
  • You want a social feed, friends and shared routines.
  • You are on Android or want a web app today.
  • You want the lowest price, or a free plan.

Where Caliber is the better choice

  • You follow a structured program and want it run for you, including a spreadsheet you already use.
  • You want each set’s load to adjust to how the day is going.
  • You train at a gym and at home on the same program.
  • You want the next block built automatically when a program ends.

Who should pick which

If you already know what to do each session and want to record it, Hevy is excellent and inexpensive. If you want the program itself handled, from today’s loads to the next six weeks, Caliber is built for that.

Frequently asked questions

Can I move from Hevy to Caliber?

Your program can come with you: rebuild it as a spreadsheet and import it into Caliber. Workout history import isn’t listed as a Caliber feature yet.

Is Caliber more expensive than Hevy?

Yes. Hevy Pro is cheaper because it is mainly a logger. Caliber includes programming and set-by-set adjustment, closer to coaching apps that cost more.

Does Caliber have a social feed?

No. Caliber focuses on the program and your training data.
